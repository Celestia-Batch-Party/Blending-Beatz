The Show

Where Heritage Meets Harmony

Blending Beatz is the grand brass band show presented by the Senior Western Boys Brass Band of Dharmapala Vidyalaya Pannipitiya. Step into an arena where the thunder of drums, the ring of brass and the spirit of the ancient warrior meet on one stage.
